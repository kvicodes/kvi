# Content claims — evidence record

Claims about KVI products that need outside evidence before they go on the
website. Researched 2026-10-19. Update this file when the founder confirms or
supplies evidence; the acceptance suite (`tests/e2e/site.mjs`) blocks the
unverified wording below from appearing on the site.

## Published (founder-confirmed facts)

- **Mokari Gobindbhog Rice** is a product offered by KVI Greens, a division of
  Kaimur Valley Innovations Pvt. Ltd. There is no separate Mokari brand or
  company.
- Premium aromatic Gobindbhog rice. Planned retail packs: **500 g and 1 kg**.
  Not yet on sale — shown as "Pre-launch".
- Gobindbhog is traditionally used in preparations such as kheer (general
  culinary fact, not a temple-specific claim).
- The product takes its name from Mokari village in Kaimur, Bihar, which press
  reports describe as known for Gobindbhog / Govind Bhog cultivation
  ([Lalluram, 2025-09-06](https://lalluram.com/mokeri-gobind-bhog-rice-farmers-fight-fertilizer-shortage/)).

Not published (no evidence supplied): prices, FSSAI licence, organic status,
certifications, lab results, capacity, partner-farm sourcing, marketplace
listings, GI status (the 2017 GI tag is for *Gobindobhog* of West Bengal, not
Kaimur rice).
